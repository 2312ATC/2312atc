## Wing sports day

Cadets compete in track, field, swimming, and team events against squadrons from across the wing.

### Events

- 100 m, 200 m, 800 m, and relay races
- Long jump and shot put
- Swimming heats (50 m freestyle and medley relay)
- Football and volleyball tournaments

### Travel

Coach departs Tavistock at **0830** — arrive in trainers and bring squadron polo shirt. Lunch provided; bring water bottle and sun cream.

Sign-up sheet on the activities board until 12 June.
