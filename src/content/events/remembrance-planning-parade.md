## Remembrance rehearsal

All cadets on the **Remembrance Sunday** parade list must attend this rehearsal two weeks before the event.

### Covered in the session

- Town centre route and timings
- Wreath party drill and escort positions
- Band tempo for slow march
- No. 1 uniform inspection

Cadets not yet measured for No. 1s should see the quartermaster at the Tuesday parade before rehearsal.

The civic parade date is confirmed with Tavistock Town Council each October — this rehearsal uses last year's timings as a baseline.
