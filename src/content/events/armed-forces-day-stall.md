## Armed Forces Day

2312 Squadron will have a stall in **Tavistock town square** as part of the local Armed Forces Day celebrations.

### Volunteer roles

- Setting up display boards and recruiting literature
- Talking to visitors about Air Cadets activities
- Assisting with drill demonstrations (scheduled 1130 and 1400)
- Packing down and returning kit to HQ

Cadets should wear No. 2 uniform with beret. Lunch rota sign-up at parade — minimum two-hour shift per volunteer.

A great opportunity for community service hours and photographs for the squadron archive!
