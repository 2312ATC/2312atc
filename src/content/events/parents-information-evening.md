## Parents information evening

New and existing parents are invited to an informal evening with the **Commanding Officer** and civilian committee chair.

### Topics covered

- Parade night routines and attendance expectations
- Summer camp, DofE, and flying opportunities
- Subscriptions, uniform, and gift aid
- Safeguarding and how we communicate with families

Tea and coffee from 1845; presentation starts at 1900. Cadets do not need to attend unless helping with refreshments.
