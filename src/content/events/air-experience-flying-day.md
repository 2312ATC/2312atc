## Air Experience Flying

Selected cadets will fly in the **Grob Tutor** with a qualified pilot instructor at RAF Benson. Sorties typically last 20–25 minutes and count toward Blue Wings progression.

### Requirements

- Minimum age 13 and under 19 on the day
- Valid ATC membership and consent form
- Smart No. 2 uniform for travel; flying suit issued on station

### Travel

Coach leaves Tavistock at **0600** — late arrivals cannot be accommodated due to airfield security. Return approximately 1900.

The manifest is published after wing confirms slots in early July.
