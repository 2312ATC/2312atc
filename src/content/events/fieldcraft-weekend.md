## Fieldcraft weekend

Cadets will deploy to **Okehampton Training Area** for a progressive fieldcraft programme over two days.

### Junior cadets (first year)

- Basher building and camp craft
- Stove safety and ration pack cooking
- Introduction to map symbols and pacing

### Senior cadets

- Day and night navigation legs
- Signals and radio procedure in the field
- Casualty evacuation scenario

Kit list issued in August. All cadets must attend the pre-camp kit inspection on the Thursday parade before the weekend.
