## Band workshop day

The squadron band is recruiting! This Saturday workshop is open to **all cadets** — whether you have played before or not.

### What to expect

- Try woodwind, brass, and percussion instruments
- Learn basic marching band drill
- Read simple notation and follow a conductor
- Informal performance for parents at 1430

Instruments are loaned for the day. If you enjoy it, weekly band practice moves to **Thursday 1800–1900** from July.

Speak to CI Music on Tuesday parade to reserve a place.
