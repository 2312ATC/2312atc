The squadron held a promotions parade on the last Thursday of April. Nineteen cadets stepped forward to receive new rank slides, from **Cadet** through to **Cadet Sergeant**.

The reviewing officer, Squadron Leader Patel from Devon & Cornwall Wing HQ, spoke about leadership as service to others — not just wearing extra chevrons. Families joined for photographs afterwards in the cadet common room.

Congratulations to everyone promoted. If you are working toward your next rank, check the syllabus requirements on Westminster and speak to your flight commander about your development plan.
