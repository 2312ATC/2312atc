Flight Lieutenant Davies, an aerospace engineer from **RAF St Mawgan**, visited the squadron for an evening STEM workshop. Cadets learned how wing shape, centre of gravity, and control surfaces affect flight.

Each team built a balsa glider, tested it in the drill hall, and adjusted trim until they achieved the longest glide. The winning design travelled over twelve metres.

STEM activities count toward the **Master Cadet** syllabus and help cadets considering engineering apprenticeships or university courses. Watch for our robotics taster session later this year.
