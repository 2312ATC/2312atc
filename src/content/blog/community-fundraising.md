Forty cadets and twelve adult volunteers spent Saturday morning bag packing at the Tavistock supermarket. Shoppers were generous — together we raised **£1,240** for squadron activities and welfare funds.

The civilian committee organised rotas and hi-vis vests; cadets were praised by store management for their politeness and smart turnout. Funds will help subsidise summer camp fees and new band instruments.

Thank you to everyone who gave up their weekend. Our next community event is the **Armed Forces Day** stall in June — sign up on the activities board.
