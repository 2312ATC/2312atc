Twenty-four cadets from 2312 Squadron travelled to **RAF Benson** for the annual Devon & Cornwall Wing flying day. Despite early-morning fog, every cadet on the manifest flew in a Grob Tutor with a qualified pilot instructor.

Between sorties, cadets toured the air traffic control tower and met serving personnel who spoke about careers in the RAF. Several cadets logged their first hours toward the **Blue Wings** syllabus.

Places for the next wing flying allocation will be confirmed at parade night. Priority goes to cadets who have maintained good attendance and completed ground school modules.
