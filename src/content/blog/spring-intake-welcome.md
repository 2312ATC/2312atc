This term we welcomed our largest spring intake in several years. Cadets have already completed their initial uniform issue, learned basic drill, and taken part in their first squadron photograph.

Staff and senior cadets have paired up with new recruits as mentors. If you joined recently and have questions about parade nights, subs, or kit, speak to your flight commander or any NCO wearing a chevron.

Our next milestone is the **Basic Cadet Training** weekend in June, where the whole intake will camp overnight and complete their first fieldcraft exercises together.
