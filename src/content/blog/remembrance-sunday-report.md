On Remembrance Sunday, 2312 Squadron formed up with veterans, sea cadets, and other community groups for the Tavistock town parade. Cadets marched in No. 1 uniform behind the squadron banner, with the band providing the slow march.

Our **Cadet Warrant Officer** laid a wreath on behalf of the squadron at the war memorial. Afterwards, cadets attended the civic service at St Eustachius Church.

Thank you to parents and supporters who came out despite the rain, and to the civilian committee for organising transport for younger cadets.
