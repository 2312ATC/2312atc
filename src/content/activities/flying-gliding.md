Air Cadets is one of the only youth organisations in the world that gives members the opportunity to fly. Through the **Air Experience Flying (AEF)** programme, cadets can take to the skies in a Grob Tutor aircraft. Senior cadets can also undertake powered flying and gliding scholarships, potentially earning a full Private Pilot Licence.
